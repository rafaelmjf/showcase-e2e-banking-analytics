# Third-party data notices

## Banco Central do Brasil open data

This project acquires data from the Banco Central do Brasil Open Data Portal,
including COSIF balance files and SGS time series.

The source databases are made available under the Open Data Commons Open Database
License (ODbL). They are not relicensed under this repository's MIT License.

- Source portal: https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/
- COSIF balance dataset: https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/dataset/ifs-balancetes
- ODbL 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Public produced works should include a notice substantially equivalent to:

> Contains information from Banco Central do Brasil open-data databases, made
> available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Full source data is acquired at runtime and is not committed to this repository.
